> Ekalavya isn’t just another ed-tech app. It’s a dynamic, credit-based learning ecosystem where quizzes, AI tutors, and collaboration meet gamification.
> 
> A scalable SaaS that transforms passive learning into **interactive, multilingual, and adaptive education experiences**.

## **Introduction**

**Ekalavya** is an **ed-tech SaaS platform** designed to make learning engaging, social, and rewarding. With **multiplayer quizzes**, **AI-driven tutors**, and **gamified credits**, it redefines digital education.

It integrates **voice-based AI companions**, **leaderboards**, and **adaptive content modules**, ensuring an immersive, student-first experience.

## **Problem Statement**

Traditional ed-tech platforms face issues like:

- **Low engagement** in passive learning models
- **Limited personalization** in quizzes and content
- **Monetization-first** design over student experience
- **Weak social elements** that discourage collaboration

> Ekalavya flips the model—prioritizing engagement, gamification, and adaptive learning.

## **Technical Stack**

|Layer|Technologies|
|---|---|
|Frontend|Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Shadcn/UI|
|Backend|Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Realtime)|
|Payments|Stripe (credit-based system, referrals)|
|AI Layer|Quizzes, voice tutors|
|Monitoring|Sentry, Custom Logging|

## **System Architecture**

- **Credit Economy**: Daily free credits, referrals, and rewards
- **Quiz Engine**: Multiplayer, real-time with Supabase Realtime
- **AI Tutors**: Multilingual, voice-enabled companions
- **Leaderboard & Gamification**: Ranking, milestones, and badges
- **Offline Quizzes**: Resilient even without connectivity

## **Core Modules and Features**

### Multiplayer Quizzes

- Real-time, credit-based quizzes
- Custom question pools & adaptive difficulty

### AI Tutors

- Voice-driven learning with multilingual support
- Personalized Q\&A and explanations

### Leaderboards & Gamification

- Daily challenges, milestones, and rewards

### Secure Auth

- Supabase Auth with OAuth/email support
- Role-based user control

### Credit Economy

- Free daily credits + referral boosts
- Stripe-powered payments for extra credits
